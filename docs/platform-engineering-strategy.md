# Platform Engineering Strategy

## Purpose

The strategy defines how the platform will improve delivery speed, standardization, reliability, and developer experience.

## Strategy Themes

- platform product thinking
- service standardization
- developer self-service
- operational resilience
- measurable productivity

## Strategy Questions

- what problem is the platform solving?
- which teams are the first customers?
- which services should be standardized?
- how will success be measured?

## Strategy Outputs

- platform vision
- service catalog priorities
- investment themes
- success metrics
