# Platform Observability

## Purpose

Platform observability ensures platform teams can see service health, workflow health, and user experience trends.

## Observable Areas

- pipeline health
- provisioning health
- platform reliability
- request bottlenecks
- service usage trends

## Review Questions

- can the team see platform failures quickly?
- can the team see bottlenecks in self-service flows?
- are platform services measured consistently?
- are alerts actionable?

## Evidence To Collect

- dashboard screenshots
- alert rules
- incident logs
- service metrics
