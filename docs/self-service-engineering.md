# Self-Service Engineering

## Purpose

Self-service engineering lets teams provision and operate approved platform capabilities without waiting for manual platform intervention.

## Core Patterns

- self-service provisioning
- service scaffolding
- policy-backed workflows
- documented approval paths
- automated guardrails

## Review Questions

- what can teams do on their own?
- where do approvals still slow work?
- which workflows should be automated next?
- how are guardrails enforced?

## Evidence To Collect

- workflow inventory
- automation coverage
- request turnaround times
- exceptions and escalations
