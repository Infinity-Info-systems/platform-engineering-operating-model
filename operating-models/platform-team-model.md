# Platform Team Model

## Purpose

The platform team model defines the roles and responsibilities needed to run a platform as a product.

## Common Roles

- platform lead
- platform engineer
- product owner
- SRE partner
- developer experience lead
- service catalog owner
- automation owner

## Operating Principles

- treat the platform as a product
- measure adoption and usefulness
- keep team responsibilities explicit

## Team Outcome

The team model should make it clear who owns the product experience, who owns the services, and who owns the reliability of the platform itself.
